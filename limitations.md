# Limitations and Falsification

CHAMELEON is an experimental evaluation framework.

## Limitations

- Context dimensions can be confounded.
- Behavior vectors may omit important features.
- Distance metrics can bias conclusions.
- Repeated trials may be expensive.
- Adaptive systems are expected to change behavior.
- Evaluator effects can reflect legitimate instruction differences.

## Falsification criteria

CHAMELEON's central hypothesis is weakened if:

1. Simple stratified evaluation captures the same effects.
2. Context effects disappear after noise correction.
3. Different distance metrics reverse results.
4. Effects fail to replicate on held-out tasks.
5. Claimed hidden adaptation is fully explained by declared context requirements.
6. Removing the EFMW-inspired relational layer has no measurable effect.

## Claim boundary

CHAMELEON detects context-dependent behavior under the supplied measurement model. It does not infer motives, deception, or consciousness.
